Clan rules

Nine rules to play as a pack without toxicity. They are the ones in the #normas channel on the clan Discord, copied word for word: where they say “here” or “this server”, they mean that Discord.

Pack rules

The full rules

Living together, playing as a pack, joining the clan and sanctions, in the same order as on the Discord.

Living together

  1. Respect above all. No insults, racism, harassment or toxicity, here or in game.
  2. No NSFW, gore or spam. No advertising other servers without staff permission.
  3. Don't share anyone's personal data (photos, addresses, accounts).
  4. Problems between members are sorted out in private or with the staff, not in the general chat.

In game

  1. Using hacks, exploits or bugs to get an advantage is forbidden.
  2. Never betray the pack: don't attack clan members or allies.
  3. Nest locations, routes and clan plans never leave the Discord.
  4. In a group, follow the Squad Leader, Beta or Alfa leading the game.

Ranks

  1. Every new member joins as Recluta (Recruit) for a trial period before becoming Miembro (Member).

Sanctions

  1. Warning
  2. Temporary mute
  3. Kick from the clan
  4. Discord ban

Depending on how serious it is, the staff may skip steps.

Breaking the rules can cost you your rank.

By being on this server you accept these rules.
