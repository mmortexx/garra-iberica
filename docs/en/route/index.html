Achievements route

The Asura achievements, session by session. Only you can see what you have on Nexus, so here you tick them by hand.

Your progress

Sign in with your Discord account to lose nothing and see it on any device. Clan server members only. We keep your Discord ID, your name, your achievements and their dates: no email, no password, no friends list, and only you see what is yours. Full details.

It works without signing in too: your ticks are saved in this browser only.

Hi, . Your ticks are saved to your account.

This deletes your account, all your achievements and their dates. It cannot be undone: download your data first if you want to keep it. Recovery copies renew within a few days (details).

Already using the route? Bring your code

Paste the code that starts with GR1. It never removes anything: it only adds what you were missing. You can also copy yours, to take it to another browser without an account.
