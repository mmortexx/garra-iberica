About the clan

Looking for a clan in The Isle Evrima?

GARRA IBÉRICA (“Iberian Claw”) is a Spanish-speaking The Isle Evrima clan. The Isle is a dinosaur survival game for PC. We are not a game server: we are a Hispanic community that organises on Discord to play together on an unofficial server. We started out in Spain and play with people from all over Latin America. Spanish is what you'll hear most in the clan, but you don't need it to join.

On the island, running as a pack changes everything: you take down prey you could never hunt alone, protect your nest and grow with someone watching your back. We look for consistent players and good teammates rather than experts: if you've just started, the veterans will teach you; if you've been playing a while, you get a regular group. And no toxicity, with clear rules.

Who runs the clan, its battles and how you rise: see The clan.
