Combat guide

How we head out to fight as a pack on the Asura server: voice, coordinates, calls and the rules nobody breaks. It is the same guide the clan keeps in the #wiki channel on Discord.

Before heading outCallsAsura rules

Step by step

Before the fight

1Before heading out

  • Join voice (Cacería 1 or 2). No mic? Type in the voice channel's chat and the bot reads it out loud.
  • In game you can only group with your own species, and the limit depends on the dino (e.g. Carnotaurus 3, Ceratosaurus 5, Omniraptor 8). If there are more of us, several packs, each with its own leader.

2Say where you are

  • Press Tab and copy your coordinates from the red box. Paste them in chat or say them on voice: "contacto en …" (contact at …).

How we talk to each other

Calls

3In-game calls

  • 1 "I'm here" (careful: enemies hear it too)
  • 2 held, invite to the group
  • 3 threaten or decline
  • 4 help.

4Clan voice calls

We say them in Spanish, so these are the four words to learn.

  • "Contacto" (contact) + where
  • "Foco en …" (focus on …) everyone on the same one
  • "Reagrupar" (regroup) back to the leader
  • "Retirada" (retreat) leave now, no arguing.

So nobody gets banned

Injuries, Asura rules and afterwards

5Injuries

  • Bleeding? Don't run: it gets worse. Once you are safe, rest (H).
  • Broken leg: pull back and rest for a few minutes.

6Asura rules

  • Exploiting map bugs is forbidden: ambushing from under the Delta bridge is punished. Getting inside the scenery (meshing) is a ban with no warning.
  • If an admin is helping someone, do not attack them.
  • No spamming nest invites.
  • The clan tag is never used to go after another clan.

7Afterwards

  • Clips go to the clips and videos channel. If it was a battle against another clan, tell a leader so it goes into #batallas.

The clan's battles, with their videos, are also on The clan. And the pack's rules, on Rules.
