Abilities

  • Climbs almost any surface (not with stamina at 0)
  • Hold RMB and Space: pounce or latch; more damage the heavier the target
  • Dives for 25 s with night vision underwater
  • Its attacks cause bleed

Tip: Attack from above and in a pack; in melee against big ones it loses.

Recommended mutations

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Accelerated Prey Drive: more damage to prey below 35% health
  • Hematophagy: you regain some thirst while eating carcasses
