Growth

Max group
5

Abilities

  • Hold Alt + LMB: directional tail swipe with the highest bleed in the game (more damage to the head)
  • Hold RMB: charge (costs stamina)
  • Alternate A and D: dodge hits to the head
  • After the tail swipe, hold S to back off

Tip: Punish anyone who comes from behind and always protect your head.

Recommended mutations

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Truculency: you shake off latched attackers sooner
  • Hypervigilance: wider camera angle while eating and drinking, and you hear footsteps better

Terms and data

How to read the cards

Terms

  • Prime: adult that met 5 requirements before 75% growth (perfect diet, sanctuary, migrations…): more weight, bonuses and one more mutation slot.
  • Frail: adult that did not reach Prime; from 87.5% it slowly gets weaker.
  • Growth: the game's base time (1×). On servers with a multiplier it is shorter.
  • Max group: the largest group size for the species.

Mutations

  • As you grow you pick mutations in slots; others unlock by completing tasks.
  • With your animal at 100% you can Entomb: you start over as a hatchling and keep your upgraded mutations (patch 0.21.321, 28 Dec 2025).
  • Slots: one as a juvenile, one as a sub-adult and one as an adult; a Prime gets a 4th. The small ones are inherited from your parents.
  • The recommended ones in each card are only a rough guide: they come from community guides.
