Abilities

  • Hold RMB: 360° venom pounce; it latches on and deals damage for 5 s
  • Each pounce adds one of 3 venom stages; with all 3 (they last 45 s), the prey takes more damage
  • If the pack pounces at the same time before the 3rd stage, the venom resets; a chirp tells you that you can pounce again
  • The venom mist hides you

Tip: Nocturnal: hit and run, always in a group.

Recommended mutations

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Accelerated Prey Drive: more damage to prey below 35% health
  • Hematophagy: you regain some thirst while eating carcasses
