Before you start

How to read the cards

Terms

  • Prime: adult that met 5 requirements before 75% growth (perfect diet, sanctuary, migrations…): more weight, bonuses and one more mutation slot.
  • Frail: adult that did not reach Prime; from 87.5% it slowly gets weaker.
  • Growth: the game's base time (1×). On servers with a multiplier it is shorter.
  • Max group: the largest group size for the species.

Mutations

  • As you grow you pick mutations in slots; others unlock by completing tasks.
  • With your animal at 100% you can Entomb: you start over as a hatchling and keep your upgraded mutations (patch 0.21.321, 28 Dec 2025).
  • Slots: one as a juvenile, one as a sub-adult and one as an adult; a Prime gets a 4th. The small ones are inherited from your parents.
  • The recommended ones in each card are only a rough guide: they come from community guides.

Up-to-date data

  • Reviewed 30 Oct 2026 · patch 0.21.772.
  • Sources: Evrima Quick Guide, theisle.info and the official The Isle website.
  • Spotted something wrong or outdated? Tell us on Discord and we will fix it.

Before the hunt

Will you be adult for the hunt?

Pick a species and the server's growth rate (if you don't know it, leave 1×, the game's own). It uses each card's growth time, playing non-stop from now.

Carnivores

Tyrannosaurus

Carnivore
Adult weight
9.3 t (Frail) · up to 12.3 t (Prime)
Growth
35 h 33 min
Max group
2

Abilities

  • Space (from sub-adult): ambush, a faster sprint for 15 s that costs no stamina (1 min cooldown)
  • Hold and release LMB: Crush, a lunging bite that pins prey under half its weight or wounded prey; then RMB to throw them
  • RMB: snout bash (hold for the strong one) · Alt + LMB: directional bite
  • Its bites break bones, but it cannot eat them
  • F + 1 (from adult): vocals

Tip: Brutal at ambushing and finishing off. Miss the Crush and you are stunned for 1 s, with a 5 s cooldown.

Recommended mutations What each one does

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Accelerated Prey Drive: more damage to prey below 35% health
  • Hemomania: more damage to bleeding targets

A rough guide: community guide (XGamingServer, undated).

Allosaurus

Carnivore
Adult weight

Growth
10 h
Max group

Abilities

  • Hold RMB: pounce with a claw swipe; causes bleed and deals more damage if it pins

  • Pins prey up to half its weight, or 3/4 if it is below 20% stamina or health (two Allos add their weight together); the prey breaks free by holding E

Tip: Wins through bleed from an ambush. Avoid long fights against the big ones.

Ceratosaurus

Carnivore
Adult weight
1450 kg (Frail) · up to 1950 kg (Prime)
Growth
6 h
Max group
5

Abilities

  • Cannot jump
  • Longest-range sense of smell in the game; bones give it all 3 nutrients
  • Its bile bite can make others vomit · hold RMB: charged bite (slows you by 10%)
  • Takes less damage while eating and while a body of 130 kg or more is within 30 m
  • RMB 3 times over a carcass: it vomits and makes it rot 5 times faster
  • Immune to bacteria

Tip: King of the carcasses: find the food before anyone else and defend it.

Recommended mutations What each one does

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Accelerated Prey Drive: more damage to prey below 35% health
  • Hematophagy: you regain some thirst while eating carcasses

Deinosuchus

Carnivore
Adult weight
9.5 t (Frail) · up to 13.5 t (Prime)
Growth
23 h 3 min
Max group
2

Abilities

  • RMB: lunge out of the water; hold it to keep your grip on the prey
  • To grab you must weigh at least twice as much as the prey (more if the prey is swimming)
  • Swims 500 m underwater at 30 km/h
  • The slowest dinosaur on land; its water drops from 100% to 0 in 10 min

Tip: Ambush from the shore. Away from the water you are easy prey.

Recommended mutations What each one does

  • Hydrodynamic: you swim faster (doubtful: one source says no mutation has given speed since October 2025)
  • Congenital Hypoalgesia: less damage from larger species
  • Reabsorption: you regain a little water in the rain or while swimming in fresh water

A rough guide: community guide (Fluxio, 19 Oct 2026).

Abilities

Troodon

Carnivore
Adult weight
60 kg (Frail) · up to 79.8 kg (Prime)
Growth
3 h 10 min
Max group

Abilities

  • The venom mist hides you

Tip: Nocturnal: hit and run, always in a group.

Gallimimus

Omnivore
Adult weight
535 kg (Frail) · up to 560 kg (Prime)
Growth
5 h 25 min
Max group
8

Abilities

  • Speed: 46.8 km/h with no diet and 55.4 km/h with all 3 nutrients
  • 1: rally call, a short speed boost for the flock (doesn't work alone)
  • Q: sniff out burrows · hold E: dig up frogs, crabs and compys
  • In a flock, those with a worse diet than the leader run faster near others

Tip: A runner: avoid melee and keep your diet up to be faster.

  • Cellular Regeneration: you heal faster
  • Congenital Hypoalgesia: less damage from larger species
  • Osteosclerosis: better resistance to fractures
