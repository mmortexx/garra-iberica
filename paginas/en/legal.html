Legal notice and privacy

Short. If you only browse, this website asks for nothing and keeps nothing. If you sign in to the achievements route with Discord, here is everything it keeps.

Who makes this website

It is made by the GARRA IBÉRICA clan, a group of The Isle Evrima players. It is a fan site and sells nothing.

For anything at all, write to us on the clan Discord (opens in a new tab).

What data of yours this website handles

  • If you only browse, it uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The exception is the achievements route, and only if you use it: see below for what it stores.
  • It has no analytics, no visit counters and no ads.
  • It has no forms. Accounts are for clan members only and are optional.It has no forms and no accounts: it asks you for no data.
  • Fonts, photos and code are on the website itself, and its security policy blocks loading anything from outside. The only exception is the videos on the clan page, which come from GitHub Pages and are only requested if you play them.
  • The clan hunt countdown and the time in each country are worked out in your browser, from its clock, without sending anything.

Achievements route accounts

Signing in is optional: without it, the route works the same and your ticks are saved in your browser only, in this website’s storage. You can delete them by clearing the site’s data.

If you sign in with Discord, we check that you are a member of the clan server and, so you can see your progress on any device, we keep:

  • your Discord ID, the name you show on the server, the date you joined and your last access;
  • the achievements you tick and the date of each one.

We do not keep your email, your password, your friends list or your servers. Discord gives us a permission that we use once and cancel straight away. Only you see what is yours: there are no profiles visible to other members. The clan’s admins can download a backup with all of the above, only so that nothing is lost if something fails.

Where it is. Cloudflare stores it, in its European Union network, on behalf of the clan. To answer each request, Cloudflare sees technical data such as your IP address and handles it under its privacy policy (opens in a new tab). The clan does not sell your data or pass it to anyone.

In your browser. Signing in uses a technical cookie for 10 minutes at most, only to complete the trip to Discord and prevent impersonation. After that, your ticks and your session key stay in this website’s storage. A session lasts 30 days. Signing out removes them from that browser.

How long. Until you delete it. Leaving the clan does not delete it: once your session expires you will not be able to sign in again, so download and delete it before you leave. If you have already left, send a private Discord message from your account to Imaginary, Quilez or Ray: within one month at most we will send you your data or delete it, whichever you ask for.

You decide. On the page itself you can download all your data and delete it with a button, at any time. Automatic recovery copies disappear within 7 days, and the backups the clan downloads are replaced by newer ones. If a copy ever has to be restored, we will announce it on the clan Discord with its date, so you can delete again anything you deleted after it. For any question or to exercise your rights, write to us on the clan Discord or by private message to those same people. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Minors. If you are under 14, do not sign in without permission from your parent or guardian. You can use the route without an account: nothing leaves your browser.

The achievements route

Your ticks are saved in your browser only, in this website’s storage: they do not leave it. You can delete them by clearing the site’s data.

Where it is hosted

On Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare may log technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, to serve and protect the website. It explains this in its privacy policy (opens in a new tab). The clan has no access to those logs.

The videos and the old address, which only brings you here, remain on GitHub Pages. GitHub handles those visits and explains this in its privacy statement (opens in a new tab).

Links to other sites

Links to Discord, Steam, Asura, Google Calendar, GitHub and Cloudflare take you off this website. Once you open them, each one’s own rules and privacy apply.
