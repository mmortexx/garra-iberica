Legal notice and privacy

Short, because this website asks you for nothing and keeps nothing about you.

Who makes this website

It is made by the GARRA IBÉRICA clan, a group of The Isle Evrima players. It is a fan site and sells nothing.

For anything at all, write to us on the clan Discord (opens in a new tab).

What data of yours this website handles

  • It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It has no analytics, no visit counters and no ads.
  • It has no forms or accounts: it asks you for no data.
  • It loads nothing from other sites: fonts, photos and code are on the website itself, and its security policy blocks loading anything from outside.
  • The clan hunt countdown and the time in each country are worked out in your browser, from its clock, without sending anything.

Where it is hosted

On GitHub Pages, a GitHub service. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, to serve and protect the website. It explains this in its privacy statement (opens in a new tab). The clan has no access to those logs.

Links to other sites

Links to Discord, Steam, Google Calendar and GitHub take you off this website. Once you open them, each one's own rules and privacy apply.
