Bring your achievements from Nexus

  1. Open your achievements list on Nexus (opens in a new tab). It looks like strange text: that is normal. If it only says “Unauthorized”, sign in to Nexus first and open it again.
  2. Copy the whole page: Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C. On a phone, press and hold the text and choose “Select all”. In Firefox, first open the second tab at the top (Raw Data).
  3. Come back here and paste it into this box: your achievements tick themselves.

What you paste is read only in your browser and that text is not sent anywhere: only the ticks that come from it are saved, in this browser and, if you are signed in, in your account. It never removes anything: it only adds what you were missing, and you can keep ticking by hand.

Already using the route? Bring your code

Paste the code that starts with GR1. It never removes anything: it only adds what you were missing. You can also copy yours, to take it to another browser without an account.
