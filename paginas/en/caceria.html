Weekly clan hunt: Saturdays at 22:00

Every Saturday at 22:00 Spanish mainland time (21:00 in the UK) the clan goes hunting together. We meet in the Cacería 1 voice channel, form a pack and a leader sets the course.

Next hunt

From Spain and Latin America

A fixed date for the whole pack

In Latin America it falls between 13:00 and 18:00, depending on the country and the time of year.

Not in the clan yet? Start with the three steps to join.

How to sign up

On Discord, open the Cacería del clan event and press “Interested”; Discord will remind you before it starts.

You need at least the Recluta (Recruit) rank to join the clan voice channels.

Add it to your calendar and it will remind you every week, in your own time zone.

How each week goes

  1. 1

    Wednesday, 20:00

    Poll in #general: “Coming to the clan hunt on Saturday?”. Answer I’m in, I’ll be late or Can’t make it. It closes when the hunt starts.

  2. 2

    Saturday, 20:00

    Two hours before, a reminder in #general for everyone with the 🔔 Cacerías role, with the voice channel and the event.

  3. 3

    Saturday, 22:00

    Everyone into the Cacería 1 voice channel. We form packs by species and a leader sets the course. Before heading out, read the combat guide.

Weekly ranking. Every week #ranking gets the clan’s table with Asura Nexus data, and the hunter of the week: whoever has the most kills.
