Weekly clan hunt: Saturdays at 22:00

Every Saturday at 22:00 Spanish mainland time (21:00 in the UK) the clan goes hunting together. We meet in the Cacería 1 voice channel, form a pack and a leader sets the course.

Spain time

How each week goes

In Latin America it falls between 13:00 and 18:00, depending on the country and the time of year. Pick your country to see every time here.

  1. 1

    Wednesday, 20:00

    Poll in #general: “Coming to the clan hunt on Saturday?”. Answer I’m in, I’ll be late or Can’t make it. It closes when the hunt starts.

  2. 2

    Saturday, 20:00

    Two hours before, a reminder in #general for everyone with the 🔔 Cacerías role, with the voice channel and the event. How to get the role.

  3. 3

    Saturday, 22:00

    Everyone into the Cacería 1 voice channel. We form packs by species and a leader sets the course. Before heading out, read the combat guide and check whether your dino will be adult in time.

Weekly ranking

What it counts
Kills, K/D, hours, quests, achievements and deaths over the last 7 days, using Asura Nexus data.
Where and when
In #ranking. It is updated as the week goes on and a new one starts every Monday.
What it hands out
The hunter of the week (whoever has the most kills), the clan’s deadliest dino and a funny title for everyone, such as King of the Food Chain (Rey de la cadena alimenticia, most kills) or Island Snack (Merienda de la isla, most deaths).
How to show up
Only members of the clan group on Asura Nexus show up: the link to join it is in #ranking.

So you don’t miss it

How to sign up

  • On the event

    On Discord, open the Cacería del clan event and press “Interested”; Discord will remind you before it starts.

  • With the 🔔 Cacerías role

    You get the two-hour reminder. On Discord, in Canales y roles → Personalizar (Channels & Roles → Customize), answer «Sí, avisadme» to «¿Te avisamos de las cacerías?» (“Shall we tell you about the hunts?”).

  • In your calendar

    It will remind you every week, in your own time zone.
