How to join the clan

Three steps inside our Discord. Joining is free: you only need to own The Isle on Steam and have a Discord account.

Join the Discord (opens in a new tab) Frequently asked questions

First steps

From zero to Recruit

  1. 1

    Join and read the rules

    Join our Discord (opens in a new tab) and read the #normas channel: being on the server means you accept them. You can also read them here on the website.

  2. 2

    Fill in the application

    In the #solicitudes channel, choose Solicitud de ingreso from the menu. The bot will ask you a few questions by direct message, so keep your DMs open.

  3. 3

    Wait for review

    The staff reviews your application. If accepted, you get the Recluta (Recruit) rank and the clan and voice channels open up.

No mic? No problem: join a voice channel and type in its chat. The bot joins by itself and reads it aloud, naming the speaker first. Use /set nick to choose what it calls you.

Now you are a Recruit

Your first week

What to do right after joining, so the pack gets to know you and you don’t miss the hunt.

  1. 1

    Introduce yourself and get the alerts

    Tell us who you are and which dinos you play in the introductions channel; every channel is in the index pinned in #bienvenida. Then, in Canales y roles → Personalizar (Channels & Roles → Customize), answer «Sí, avisadme» to get the 🔔 Cacerías role.

  2. 2

    Add us on Asura Nexus

    It is a website for Evrima players, with a live map and dino storage; the clan ranking data comes from it. Post your profile link in #perfiles so we can all add each other, and join the clan group on Nexus (the link is in #ranking): that is the only way to show up in the weekly ranking.

  3. 3

    Come to the Saturday hunt

    Answer Wednesday’s poll in #general and get your dino ready with its guide entry and the combat guide. See you in Cacería 1: time and countdown.

From Recruit to Member. After one or two active weeks, following the rules and playing in a group. The staff decides promotions and you don’t ask for them: every rank is on The clan.

The server

What’s on the Discord

This is how our Discord is organised (in Spanish). The pack and voice channels open once the staff accepts you as a Recruit.

  • Information

    #bienvenida to start, #normas (rules), #anuncios with official news (ranks and promotions pinned), #solicitudes to apply and a channel to introduce yourself.

  • The pack

    #general to chat, clan ideas and votes, #wiki with every dino’s guide, clips, #galería (gallery), nests and defence shifts, #ranking, #perfiles (profiles) and #batallas (battles).

  • Voice

    Cacería 1 and another channel for hunting groups, carnivore, herbivore and human games, a quiet chat, one to cry over your deaths and an AFK one.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Before you join

How do I join the clan?

Join our Discord, read the rules, fill in the application from the bot menu and wait for the staff to review it. If accepted, you start as a Recruit. The three steps are explained above.

Are you recruiting new players?

Yes, recruitment is open. We look for consistent players and good teammates; experience comes from playing with the pack.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. Spanish is what you'll hear most in the clan, but it isn't a requirement. Our Discord's main posts come in Spanish and English, and the combat guide teaches you the four calls we use.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining the clan and the Discord is free. You only need to own The Isle on Steam (opens in a new tab).

I'm new to The Isle. Can I join?

Yes. The clan's veterans help and guide recruits, and you learn much faster in a group.

Do I need a microphone?

No. You can type in the voice channel chat and a bot reads it out loud, naming the speaker first.

Can I join from Latin America?

Yes. The clan started in Spain, but it is open to any player, wherever they play from.

Is GARRA IBÉRICA a server for The Isle?

No. We are a clan: a community of players who organise on Discord to play The Isle Evrima together on an unofficial server.

What is The Isle Evrima?

The Isle is a survival game for PC, on Steam, where you play as a carnivore, herbivore or omnivore dinosaur. Evrima is its current version and Legacy the old one. The clan plays Evrima.

Which server do you play on?

On Asura, an unofficial The Isle Evrima server. Its combat rules, which nobody in the clan breaks, are in the combat guide.

In the clan

When is the clan hunt?

Every Saturday at 22:00 Spanish mainland time (21:00 in the UK), in the Cacería 1 voice channel. In Latin America it falls between 13:00 and 18:00, depending on the country and the time of year; the exact time in your country is on the clan hunt page. Press “Interested” on the Discord event and Discord will remind you.

How do I hear about each hunt?

Get the 🔔 Cacerías role: on the Discord, in Canales y roles → Personalizar (Channels & Roles → Customize), answer «Sí, avisadme». On Saturday, two hours before, the reminder in #general mentions you. On Wednesday the poll to say whether you are coming is posted there.

Can I play as both a carnivore and a herbivore?

Yes. The clan has players of both types, and we have a guide for each dinosaur with recommended mutations where there is a reliable source.

How do I show up in the weekly ranking?

Join the clan group on Asura Nexus: the link is in the #ranking channel. The ranking covers the last 7 days and a new one starts every Monday; it is explained on the clan hunt page.

How do I rank up?

By playing. Time counts, but above all how you play and how you treat the pack. Promotions are decided by the staff, so don't ask for them. See the ranks summary.

Can I support the clan?

We don't accept money or donations of any kind. If you want to and can, the only thing that helps is a server boost (Discord boost) on our Discord (opens in a new tab): boosts unlock more emojis and stickers, better voice quality and the server banner. Boosters automatically get the server booster role.
